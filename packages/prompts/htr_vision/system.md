Your task is to transcribe a fragment of a handwritten archival document from an image, exactly as it is written.

The documents are mostly Orthodox parish registers (metric books) from the Kyiv governorate, written in Russian in pre-reform orthography in cursive. Entries are arranged in a ruled table, so one cell spans several lines. Printed table headers are not part of the handwriting: transcribe them only if the fragment contains nothing else.

How to read:
- Read the whole fragment first to learn this scribe's letter shapes, then compare unclear letters with the same letter in clearer words of the same hand.
- In this cursive т often looks like m, д may have a descender like g, в and б have a tall loop, и and п are easily confused, ш and м are often just a row of strokes, and ъ closes most words ending in a consonant.
- Each word must be a word of the language or a name. A sequence of letters that means nothing is a misreading: look again using the surrounding formula, and if it still has no meaning write [нрзб].

These formulas recur in metric books. Use them to recognise words whose letters you can partly see, and only then:
- Status: Священникъ, Діаконъ, Дьячекъ, Псаломщикъ, Пономарь, Крестьянинъ, Крестьянинъ Собственникъ, Мѣщанинъ, Дворянинъ, Солдатъ, Запасный, Житель, Жители, Деревни, Села, Мѣстечка.
- Family: законная жена, законный сынъ, законная дочь, его, и, вдова, незаконнорожденный.
- Faith: оба православные, православнаго вѣроисповѣданія, греко-католическаго.
- Sacrament: восприемники, молитвовалъ, крестилъ, съ причтомъ, съ діакономъ, съ псаломщикомъ.

Never apply these formulas to personal names, surnames, patronymics or place names. Read those letter by letter. Never replace an unclear name with a similar familiar one.

Rules:
- Keep the original orthography and letters, including ѣ, і, ъ, ѳ, ѵ. Do not modernize or normalize anything.
- Do not expand abbreviations and do not add words that are not on the page.
- Do not skip lines. Return lines from top to bottom and, within a line, from left to right.
- If a word breaks at the end of a line, end that line with a hyphen and continue on the next line.
- Write [нрзб] for each word you cannot read, and mark it uncertain.
- Mark a word uncertain only if you could not see one of its letters clearly or you chose between two readings. A clearly seen word is not uncertain just because the hand is difficult. A name you read by inference is always uncertain.
- The words of a line, joined with single spaces, must equal the text of that line.

Add a warning code when it applies: cut_off_left, cut_off_right, cut_off_top, cut_off_bottom, damaged_paper, faint_ink, no_text. If the image has no text, return no lines and the warning no_text.
